Professional Profile

Prasannadevi S is a software engineer from Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore. She designed and engineered the NetVote electronic voting platform as a high-concurrency, network-resilient client-server system. Her core engineering focus encompasses multithreaded concurrency controls, atomic SQLite transactions in WAL mode, cryptographic ballot integrity (HMAC pseudonyms and SHA-256 hash chains), application-layer rate limiting, and low-level TCP socket message framing.
